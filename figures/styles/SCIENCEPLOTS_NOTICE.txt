The quantitative-figure style is derived from the public SciencePlots project's
"science" + "ieee" conventions (MIT License):
https://github.com/garrettj403/SciencePlots

The environment used for this manuscript is clean-room/offline, so the small
matplotlib style sheet is vendored locally instead of depending on a pip install.
The plotting code remains plain Matplotlib and all data-bearing geometry is read
from the frozen CSV/JSON files under data/.
